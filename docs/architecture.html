OpenSport architecture

A personal, Strava-like platform. A backend on Railway syncs Garmin activities on its own, keeps the original FIT files and computes its own advanced metrics. Two clients use it: first a PWA for desktop and mobile, hosted on Vercel, and later an Android app. English by default, Spanish available.

How a sync works

1 · Trigger

Railway's cron every 15 min, or the "Sync" button in the PWA or the app (POST /api/sync). The PWA polls progress every 2 s.

2 · Garmin

Key → 24 h JWT → new activities → download-service/files/activity/{id} → ZIP → original FIT to the volume.

3 · Processing

Parser → zones, effort, GAP, records and power curve → Postgres. Then fitness and freshness are recomputed incrementally.

4 · Notification

Notification (Web Push / FCM): "6.6 km run · 1 km record", in your language. Clients refresh and cache for offline use.

Advanced metrics (the "Premium" ones, built by us)

Effort and fitness

  • HR and pace zones with your settings
  • Relative effort (TRIMP)
  • Fitness, fatigue and form (CTL / ATL / TSB)

Performance

  • Grade-adjusted pace (GAP)
  • Records from 400 m to marathon and their progression
  • Race prediction (Riegel)
  • Power curve for rides and ROUVY

Routes and volume

  • Matched routes and time comparison
  • Personal heatmap
  • Volume and load per week, month and year
  • Garmin data: Training Effect, later HRV and sleep

Decisions

Garmin Personal API

Official, for individuals, no approval needed. Its FIT is byte-for-byte identical to Garmin's "Export File". Strava's is missing 11 proprietary message types.

Railway

Always available for the cron and notifications. Postgres, volume and cron in one project, about $5/month. A standard container allows moving to Fly.io or a VPS.

PWA first, on Vercel

One codebase for desktop and mobile, the fastest way to validate, with a preview per PR. The /api proxy avoids CORS and cookie problems. The native app comes when reliable notifications, background work and Health Connect are needed.

Discarded

  • Strava API: paid since June 2026
  • Automating websites: bot checks and terms of use
  • intervals.icu: Garmin access uncertain
  • Garmin app on the phone: needs root

Phases

1 · Backend on Railway

opensport package, Postgres, volume, auth, activities API and cron.

2 · PWA on Vercel

Desktop and mobile: home, detail, search, Sync button, themes, English and Spanish, installable and offline.

3 · Metrics

Zones, TRIMP, fitness, GAP, records, power, routes, heatmap, estimates.

4 · Notifications and history

Web Push, Strava ZIP import, privacy zones and sharing.

5 · Android app

React Native + Expo on the shared code. Starts with a performance proof of concept.

6 · Health

Sleep and HRV from Garmin, Google Health (Fitbit Air) and Health Connect.

7 · AI assistant

Summaries, weekly report and Coach with Claude Haiku 5.5.
